# energy_analysis

Sixty Today in Energy articles from the U.S. Energy Information Administration, taken
evenly across the 156 articles from 2024 that carry an oil, natural gas, refining, or
related tag.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Source | U.S. Energy Information Administration, eia.gov/todayinenergy |
| Retrieved | 2026-11-02 |
| Terms read | 2026-11-02, https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php |
| Attribution | "Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration (Mon YYYY)", on every document |
| Verdict | **Publishable** (srd017 R9.7) |

## Terms and reasoning

EIA states: "U.S. government publications are in the public domain and are not subject
to copyright protection", and asks for an acknowledgment that includes the publication
date.

EIA also says its site can carry material "contributed or licensed by private
individuals, companies, or organizations", which that statement does not cover. So this
tier takes article text only and leaves images and charts behind, and the fetch refuses
an article whose text carries a third-party rights notice. None was refused on the last
fetch.

EIA credits third-party data in many articles, naming sources such as Bloomberg and
Refinitiv. That credit is kept in each document's `data_source`. EIA's sentences report
figures from that data, and no third-party text is reproduced.

This is a judgment about reuse and not legal advice.
