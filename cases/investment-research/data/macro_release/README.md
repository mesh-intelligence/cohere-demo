## Terms and reasoning

The Board states: "Unless otherwise indicated, information on Board's website is in the
public domain and may be copied and distributed without permission", and asks to be
cited as the source.

BLS states that "everything that we publish, both in hard copy and electronically, is
in the public domain, except for previously copyrighted photographs and illustrations",
and asks to be cited. This tier carries text and no photographs or illustrations.

This is a judgment about reuse and not legal advice.
